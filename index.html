Questions

What's included in the $90?

Your custom yard greeting sign plus up to 5 flair pieces — birthday, baby, sports, grad, you name it. All flair is included at no extra cost.

How many days do I get?

The $90 price includes 2 days in your yard. Need it longer? Extra days are +$10 each, up to 5 days total: 2 days $90, 3 days $100, 4 days $110, 5 days $120.

When do you set up?

We set up the night before your event, so everything is ready and waiting first thing in the morning.

When do I pay?

You pay after it's up — once your sign is in the yard. We take it down after your event.

Which towns do you serve? Is there a travel fee?

What color are the letters?

All wording is in black letters. “Happy Birthday” also comes in color: blue, green, red, purple, pink or multicolor glitter (each letter a different sparkly color).

Can I pick more than one flair piece?

Yes! Pick up to 5 pieces, and every one is included in your $90.

How do I reach you?

Text 239-494-2377 — that's the only way to reach us, and we'll text you right back.
