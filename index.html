FAQ

Good questions

What's included in the $90?

Everything: a personalized sign with the name, age, colors and theme you want, all the flair you pick, setup the night before, 2 days in the yard, and pickup. Same price for every sign type. No travel fee except Lehigh Acres (+$15).

Can I keep it up longer?

Yes. Extra days are +$10/day, up to 5 days total: 3 days is $100, 4 days is $110, 5 days is $120. In Lehigh Acres add the $15 travel fee ($105, $115, $125, $135 for 2 to 5 days). We pick it up after your last day.

When do you set up and pick up?

We set it up the night before your event, so it's there when you wake up. $90 covers 2 days in the yard (your event day and the next day), then we pick it up. You don't need to be home.

How do I pay?

Nothing up front. You pay after it's up, by cash, Zelle or Venmo. No deposit. No travel fee except Lehigh Acres (+$15).

How much flair can I pick?

As much as you like. All flair is included in the $90, so picking more never changes the price.

What areas do you cover?

Estero, Bonita Springs, Fort Myers, San Carlos Park, Three Oaks and Gateway, with no travel fee. Lehigh Acres is +$15 travel. Cape Coral is coming in December (not bookable yet). We don't serve Naples or Fort Myers Beach. Somewhere else nearby? Text us and ask.

How do I reach you?

Text 239-494-2377 anytime. We're text only, so that's the fastest way to get an answer, a last-minute booking, or a change to your order.

How far ahead should I book?

The sooner the better, since weekends fill up. Need it last minute? Text us and we'll do our best.

Can it be a surprise?

Absolutely. Since we set it up the night before, it's waiting when they wake up. Add "it's a surprise" in the notes and we'll only contact you.

Will it hurt my lawn or sprinklers?

No. The signs go on small stakes pushed a few inches into the ground. If you have sprinkler heads or lines to avoid, mention them in the notes.

What if the weather is bad?

If a storm is headed our way, we'll text you before setup to work out the best plan.
