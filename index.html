Your custom yard greeting sign plus any flair pieces you want — characters, sports, numbers, you name it. All flair is included at no extra cost, for any number of pieces.

The $90 price includes 2 days in your yard. Need it longer? Extra days are +$10 each, up to 5 days total: 2 days $90, 3 days $100, 4 days $110, 5 days $120.

We set up the night before your event, so everything is ready and waiting first thing in the morning.

You pay after it's up — once your sign is in the yard. We take it down after your event.
